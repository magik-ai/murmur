# Flock

The murmuration, drawn live: `Murmur.flock(canvas, {count, speed, seed})`.

Every member is a bird: two crescent wings on a small body. It moves the way real starlings do, after the StarDisplay model (Hildenbrandt, Carere and Hemelrijk 2010) and the field measurements behind it. A bird holds a near constant speed and never changes direction by being pushed: it turns only by banking, rolling in twice as fast as it rolls out, so a turn is an unhurried arc of two or three seconds. It copies its seven nearest neighbours (the rule measured on real starlings), ignoring the ones behind it, keeps clear of the closest and drifts toward the rest, harder at the border than inside. It reacts about fifteen times a second rather than every frame, so a turn travels across the flock neighbour by neighbour, and a banked bird shows more wing and reads darker, so every turn rolls a dark band across the flock. Wings beat in short bursts and glide between. The only randomness is a slow drift in the bank each bird wants, and the roost the flock wheels around drifts on a minute-long loop. One bird is lit by the lamp. It reads its colours from `--ink` and `--lamp`, so it follows the theme; it runs on a fixed clock (the same pace on a 60 Hz and a 120 Hz screen), stops when it leaves the screen and draws one still frame under reduced motion.

Options: `spacing` (1 a dense winter flock, 2 an airy one over a page), `birdScale` (1.5 for a hero seen close), `roostX`, `roostY`, `roostReach` (the ellipse the flock wheels around, so a page can keep it clear of its headline), `hold` (how tightly it keeps to that ellipse: 0.3 wheels wide, 2 stays close, for a hero that must not touch the page's edge), `avoid` (a box or a list of boxes the flock banks around well before it reaches them, the page's words), `inspect` (point at a bird and lamp lines show the seven it watches). The handle can `pause()` and `play()`: a hero flock carries a Pause button (WCAG 2.2.2).

Use it once per page at most: the landing hero, an empty Board, the night report. Pair it with a plain readout ("360 birds, each watching its 7 nearest"). Never behind text a person must read, never as a spinner.
